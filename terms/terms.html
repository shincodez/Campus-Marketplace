Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Who can use the marketplace

You must be a currently enrolled student to create an account. The details you give us — your name, school email and student ID — must be true and your own.

You are responsible for your account. Keep your password private and log out on shared computers.

2. Listing items

You may only list items that you own and are allowed to sell. Describe the item honestly, use real photos of it, and state its condition and price clearly.

Mark an item as sold, or remove it, once it is no longer available.

3. Items you may not sell

The following are not allowed:

  • Weapons, explosives or anything dangerous
  • Alcohol, tobacco, vapes, drugs or prescription medicine
  • Stolen, counterfeit or pirated goods
  • Exam answers, graded work or anything that helps cheat
  • Adult content, or anything illegal or against school rules

4. Buying, payment and meetups

Purchases are agreements between students. NORSU Campus Marketplace does not take payments, hold money, ship items or guarantee any sale.

A purchase request is a message to the seller, not a payment. Agree on the price and a meetup in Messages, meet in a public place on campus, and check the item before you pay.

5. Messages and behaviour

Be respectful. Do not send spam, scams, harassment or threats. Do not ask for or share passwords, bank details or other private information.

We may remove listings or messages, or close accounts, that break these terms.

6. Your information

We store your account details, your listings and your messages so the marketplace works. Your favorites and cart are kept in your own browser.

We do not sell your information. Other students can see your name on your listings and in messages you send them.

7. No guarantees

The marketplace is provided as it is. We are not responsible for the items, the people you deal with, or any loss or dispute between students. Use your own judgement and follow the safety tips on the About page.
