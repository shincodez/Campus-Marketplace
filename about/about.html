About

NORSU Campus Marketplace is where students buy, sell and trade school things with each other — books, calculators, uniforms, gadgets and more — and meet safely on campus.

1. Find what you needBrowse the Marketplace or search by name, category or campus spot.
2. Post what you don'tList an item with photos in a few steps. Every student can see it right away.
3. Message the sellerAsk questions, agree on the price and send a purchase request from your cart.
4. Meet on campusHand off the item in a public campus place, like the Library or Student Center.

Who can use it

Accounts are for students. Sign up with your school email and student ID, then log in with either one.

Stay safe

  • Meet in busy, public places on campus and in daylight when you can.
  • Check the item before you pay. Pay in person; don't send money ahead to people you don't know.
  • Keep the conversation in Messages so there's a record of what you agreed.
  • If something feels wrong, walk away and don't share personal details.
